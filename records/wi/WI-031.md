# Archiving a Closed Study Folder

## Steps

1. Confirm the study is closed in the log.
2. Check the folder against the archive checklist.
3. Compress the folder and record its checksum.
4. Move it to the archive share and add an entry to the archive log (REC-053).
5. Remove the working copy only after the checksum is verified.
