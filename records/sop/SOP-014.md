# Data Retention and Archiving

## 1 Purpose

Set how long study and quality records are kept and how they are archived.

## 2 Scope

All controlled records created by Northwind Labs staff.

## 3 Procedure

1. Classify the record against the retention schedule (REC-052).
2. Keep active records in the working folder until the study is closed.
3. Move closed records to the archive within 30 days, following WI-031.
4. Destroy records only after the retention period and with Quality approval.

## 4 Responsibilities

Record owners classify and archive. Quality approves destruction.

## Change history

- Retention period for training records changed to 6 years (revision 2).
