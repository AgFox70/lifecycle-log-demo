# Supplier Qualification

## 1 Purpose

Decide whether a supplier is fit to provide a service to Northwind Labs.

## 2 Procedure

1. Send the supplier questionnaire (WI-032).
2. Score the answers against the evaluation criteria.
3. Record the decision in a Supplier Evaluation record.
4. Re-evaluate every 24 months or after a serious incident.
